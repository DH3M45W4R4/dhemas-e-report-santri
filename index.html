About Dhema's E-Report Santri

E-Report Santri is a web-based reporting system designed to simplify the management of student reports. Featuring report statistics and admin settings, this system helps efficiently monitor and analyze reports.

  • Report Statistics Feature
  • Admin Settings
  • User-Friendly Interface

Our Services

Submit all your issues or concerns at Dhema's E-Report Santri, which provides services to receive, understand, and resolve reports categorized into Minor, Major, and Critical levels to easily and effectively improve facility and environment quality. You can also submit reports anonymously.

Minor

Daily personal or light facility issues that do not significantly disrupt learning activities and can be solved independently or with help from fellow students.

Major

Facility, environmental, or disciplinary issues that disrupt daily comfort and activities, requiring direct repair or action from supervisors/mentors.

Critical

Urgent issues threatening physical safety, mental well-being, security, or school operations, requiring immediate handling by mentors, teachers, or management.

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions asked by users of Dhema's E-Report Santri.

Dhema's E-Report Santri is a digital platform designed to facilitate attendance and activity reporting for students in boarding schools. This platform allows administrators and supervisors to track student data efficiently in real time.

To create a new report, you need to log into your account first. After that, navigate to the "Create Report" menu and fill out the provided form with the required information.

You can access your submitted reports by logging into your account and navigating to the "My Reports" menu. There, you will see a list of all reports you have created.
